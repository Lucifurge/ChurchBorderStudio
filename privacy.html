Privacy information.

Last updated: October 2026

Website

This is a static product website. The included website source does not implement user accounts, newsletter sign-up, advertising pixels, or analytics. If the site is hosted by a third-party provider, that provider may process standard server logs as part of delivering the website; review that host’s privacy terms for details.

Pro checkout and licensing

If you purchase Pro, the email address you submit on the pricing page is sent to PayMongo as checkout metadata and used by the licensing backend to deliver your signed license through the configured transactional email provider. PayMongo processes payment information under its own privacy terms; the publisher should not receive or store complete card credentials. The first licensing version does not maintain a customer account database, but the payment provider, email provider, hosting provider, and their operational logs may process information required to complete or support a purchase.

Do not email payment card details or share your license publicly. The license key contains a purchase identifier and email address in signed form so the desktop app can verify it offline.

Desktop application

Church Border Studio is designed for local use alongside OBS Studio and FreeShow. Its local controller binds to the loopback address (127.0.0.1) and does not require a cloud account for normal operation. App settings are stored on the Windows machine.

OBS connection details

OBS WebSocket credentials are kept locally on the Windows machine. The application documentation states that the saved password is not encrypted. Do not share your settings folder or files that may contain connection details.

Downloads

When a visitor downloads an installer, the hosting provider may record download requests in its server logs. This behavior depends on the hosting provider and cannot be controlled by this static website.

Third-party software

OBS Studio, FreeShow, Windows, and hosting services are separate products with their own policies and licenses. This website does not imply endorsement by those developers.

Contact

Before public sales, the publisher must replace this notice with a working support email or contact URL for purchase, privacy, refund, and license-recovery requests.
